## Executive Summary

IWMI can broaden an equity portfolio, but small-cap liquidity, country exposure, and currency movements can materially affect realized outcomes.

## Strategy and Business Model

IWMI provides international small-cap equity exposure. The return opportunity is broader than mega-cap benchmarks, but the portfolio is more sensitive to liquidity, local economies, currency moves and index turnover.

## Evidence

Evidence should focus on country breadth, sector concentration, trading spreads, tracking difference and the risk-adjusted total return contribution to a broader portfolio.

## Key Risks

- Small-cap liquidity can disappear during market stress.
- Currency and country risk can dominate company-level fundamentals.
- Market data and fund classifications can change; verify current official disclosures.

## Analyst Conclusion

IWMI is a satellite diversification tool requiring a long horizon and disciplined sizing. The thesis weakens if implementation costs or liquidity risk consume the diversification benefit.

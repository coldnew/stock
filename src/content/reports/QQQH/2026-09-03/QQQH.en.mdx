## Executive Summary

QQQH should be compared with plain Nasdaq-100 exposure using total return, drawdown, and distribution composition over the same period.

## Strategy and Business Model

QQQH combines Nasdaq-100 exposure with an income-oriented option overlay. The portfolio retains technology and growth-equity risk while trading away some upside participation for option premium and distributions.

## Evidence

Evidence should compare NAV total return, upside and downside capture, drawdown, option coverage and distribution composition with plain QQQ over the same period.

## Key Risks

- Distributions can vary or include return of capital.
- A strong technology rally can expose the cost of capped upside.
- Fees, spreads and tax treatment affect realized results.

## Analyst Conclusion

QQQH is an income-versus-growth trade, not a superior version of QQQ by definition. The thesis is weakened when the cash-flow benefit does not compensate for lost upside and higher implementation complexity.
